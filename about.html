About Ajanta Studio

Ajanta Studio is building next-generation procedural vector graphics and motion design software. We blend the power of non-destructive mathematical recipes with the tactile intuition of marionette string bone rigging.

Our Core Innovations

Most 2D animation tools force artists to choose between rigid skeletal rigs or tedious frame-by-frame point manipulation. Ajanta unifies both with Marionette Strings bone animation and fully non-destructive, animatable recipes that keep every operation live, editable, and keyframeable.
