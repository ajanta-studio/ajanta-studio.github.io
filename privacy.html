Privacy Policy

LAST UPDATED: AUGUST 2026 // AJANTA STUDIO PRIVACY COMPLIANCE

At Ajanta Studio (accessible from https://ajanta-studio.github.io), the privacy of our visitors and software users is of paramount importance. This Privacy Policy outlines the types of information collected and recorded by Ajanta Studio and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

Ajanta Studio does not require user registration or account creation for public website access. When interacting with our websites and software services, standard diagnostic telemetry (browser type, operating system, referrer URL, timestamps) may be automatically logged to monitor platform availability.

2. Cookies and Web Beacons

Like any other website, Ajanta Studio uses cookies to store information including visitors' preferences and the pages on the website that the visitor accessed or visited. This information is used to optimize the user experience.

3. Google DoubleClick DART Cookie & AdSense Disclosures

Google is a third-party vendor on our site. It uses cookies, known as DART cookies, to serve ads to our site visitors based upon their visit to this site and other sites on the internet.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to your website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to your users based on their visit to your sites and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

4. Advertising Partners & VAST/IMA Video Beacons

Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technologies like cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons that are used in their respective advertisements and links that appear on Ajanta Studio (including our video ad player). They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. These technologies are used to measure the effectiveness of their advertising campaigns and/or to personalize the advertising content that you see.

5. CCPA & GDPR Data Protection Rights

We ensure that all users are fully aware of all of their data protection rights:

  • The right to access, rectification, and erasure of personal data.
  • The right to restrict processing and object to processing.
  • The right to data portability.

6. Contact Us

If you have additional questions or require more information about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at: privacy@ajanta.studio.
