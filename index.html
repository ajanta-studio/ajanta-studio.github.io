Marionette Strings Bone Rigging

An intuitive, tactile approach to 2D skeletal animation. Attach marionette strings to vector anchor clusters and puppet bones, enabling fluid inverse kinematics and lifelike character motion with zero tedious manual point keyframing.

⚙

Fully Non-Destructive, Animatable Recipes

A parametric recipe stack where every Boolean operation, contour offset, bevel, and path deformation remains 100% non-destructive, re-orderable, and directly keyframeable across the timeline for evolving vector shapes.

🎨

Freeform Gradient Fills

Place multi-point gradient stops freely inside complex vector paths. The continuous mesh-interpolation engine smoothly blends colors, opacities, and chromatic transitions across any geometric contour in real time.

✦

Procedural Properties

Drive any vector attribute—stroke thickness, corner radius, dash offsets, or opacity—using mathematical expressions, harmonic oscillators, and parametric controllers for generative, responsive design.

⚡

Procedural Material Shaders

Apply real-time GPU fragment shaders directly to vector fills and strokes. Render dynamic metallic reflections, physical glass refractions, iridescence, and procedural noise fields running at 120 FPS.

∿

Multi-Track Motion Timeline

Ultra-responsive keyframing with integrated velocity graphs, spatial motion paths, and custom cubic-bezier easing curves. Export animations to SVG, Lottie, and high-framerate video formats seamlessly.
